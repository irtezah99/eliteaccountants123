FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what we're asked most often. Still have a question? Get in touch.

How Does Buying A Presto Homes Property Work?

Presto Homes builds premium new homes on spec — on our own initiative, not to custom order. Once a home is complete, we open it up for inspection at scheduled open house events, then sell it to the highest bidder on auction day. There's no waiting on a design slot and no negotiation queue.

How Do I Register To Bid At An Auction?

Contact our team ahead of auction day to register as a bidder. You'll need photo ID and, in most cases, proof of finance or funds. We'll confirm your registration and send you the auction terms before the day.

How Do I Book An Open House Visit?

Open house times for each available home are listed on that home's project page. Reach out via our Contact page and we'll confirm the next session and add you to updates for that property.

Do I Need My Own Agent To Buy A Presto Home?

No — you can register, inspect, and bid directly with our team. We'll guide you through every step, from your first open house to final settlement, with no agent commissions involved.

Can I Customise The Floor Plan Or Finishes?

Because homes are built speculatively before they're sold, floor plans and finishes are fixed by the time a home goes to open house. What you see at inspection is exactly what you're bidding on.

What's Included In The Purchase Price?

The price covers the completed home and land, plus all fixtures, fittings, and finishes shown at inspection — flooring, cabinetry, appliances, and landscaping included. Furniture and styling shown at open house are not included unless stated.

Do I Need Finance Pre-Approval Before Auction Day?

Yes, strongly recommended. Auction sales are typically unconditional, so it's important to have finance pre-approved before you register to bid. Use the Mortgage Calculator on any project page to estimate repayments while you plan.

Can I Get A Building And Pest Inspection Before Bidding?

Yes. We encourage every prospective bidder to arrange an independent building and pest inspection during the open house period, ahead of auction day — just let our team know and we'll help coordinate access.

What Deposit Is Required On Auction Day?

A 10% deposit is payable immediately when the hammer falls, with the balance due at settlement. Specific terms are confirmed in the contract of sale provided before the auction.

How Long Does Settlement Take After The Auction?

Standard settlement is typically 30–42 days after auction day, though this can vary by contract. Our team will confirm the exact settlement date as part of your auction registration pack.

Is There A Warranty Or After-Sale Support Once I Move In?

Every Presto home is covered by a structural building warranty from settlement. Our services team also offers landscaping, fencing, and home repair or renovation work after you've moved in — see the Services page for details.

What Areas Do You Build In?

We build across Sydney and surrounding NSW suburbs. If you're unsure whether a home is within reach of you, just ask — our team can point you to current and upcoming open house locations.
