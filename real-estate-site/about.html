About Horizon Realty Group

Eighteen years of building homes and building trust, one neighborhood at a time.

Our Story

From a two-person office to a full-service brokerage

Horizon Realty Group was founded in 2008 by two agents who believed real estate should be transparent, local, and a little less stressful. Today we're a full-service brokerage handling residential sales, commercial leasing, property management and post-move home services — but the founding principle hasn't changed: agents who actually live in the neighborhoods they sell.

We've since helped over 1,200 families find a home and closed more than 320 commercial transactions, while keeping the same hands-on, no-pressure approach we started with.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what we're asked most often. Still have a question? Get in touch.

How much does it cost to work with an agent?

For buyers, our services are typically at no direct cost to you — the commission is paid by the seller at closing. For sellers, we charge a competitive commission that's agreed upfront with no hidden fees.

Do you help with new-build projects as well as resales?

Yes. Horizon lists both newly built homes from our development partners and resale properties, so you can compare options side by side.

Can Horizon help with post-purchase renovations?

Absolutely — our services team offers landscaping, fencing, and home repair or renovation work for properties we've sold, available on request. See the Services page for details.

What areas do you cover?

We operate across the greater metro area, including surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether a location is in our coverage area, just ask — we can usually connect you with a trusted partner agent even outside our core territory.

How do I get a mortgage estimate?

Use the Mortgage Calculator link in the site navigation (or on any project page) to get an instant estimate of your monthly payment based on price, down payment, interest rate and term.

How long does a typical sale take?

From listing to closing, most residential sales take 30–60 days depending on financing and inspection timelines. Commercial transactions vary more widely based on deal complexity.
