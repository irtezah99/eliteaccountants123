Man Behind the Success

Mr. Farhat Jaffri

Founder & CEO, Presto Homes | Director, FJ Group of Companies

Presto Homes is a proud venture of the FJ Group of Companies, founded by Mr. Farhat Jaffri — a self-made entrepreneur with a genuinely futuristic vision for real estate. Through tireless dedication, strong work ethics, and a passion for innovation, he has built Presto Homes into one of Sydney's most distinguished home builders in under a decade — a rare achievement built on calculated risk-taking and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Beyond Presto Homes, Mr. Jaffri is also Director of Elite Accountants (eliteaccountants.com.au). A former President of the Pakistani Association of Australia (PAA), he now serves as President of the Federation of Pakistan Australia — a role he has embraced with even greater energy and vision. The resounding success of Azaadi Mela 2026 stands as a testament to his continued dedication to Sydney's Pakistani community.

An avid cricketer, Mr. Jaffri played domestic cricket in Karachi, Pakistan, and today lends his expertise as a cricket analyst during major tournaments.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what we're asked most often. Still have a question? Get in touch.

How much does it cost to work with an agent?

For buyers, our services are typically at no direct cost to you — the commission is paid by the seller at closing. For sellers, we charge a competitive commission that's agreed upfront with no hidden fees.

Do you help with new-build projects as well as resales?

Yes. Presto Homes lists both newly built homes from our development partners and resale properties, so you can compare options side by side.

Can Presto Homes help with post-purchase renovations?

Absolutely — our services team offers landscaping, fencing, and home repair or renovation work for properties we've sold, available on request. See the Services page for details.

What areas do you cover?

We operate across the greater metro area, including surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether a location is in our coverage area, just ask — we can usually connect you with a trusted partner agent even outside our core territory.

How do I get a mortgage estimate?

Use the Mortgage Calculator link in the site navigation (or on any project page) to get an instant estimate of your monthly payment based on price, down payment, interest rate and term.

How long does a typical sale take?

From listing to closing, most residential sales take 30–60 days depending on financing and inspection timelines. Commercial transactions vary more widely based on deal complexity.
